    HOW TO PLAY
    CONTROLS — DESKTOP
    ↑↓←→Move your player
    W / SHIFTSprint — burns stamina, jogging restores it
    A / I / SPACEPASS when you have the ball · SWITCH when defending
    S / KSHOOT (hold to charge) when attacking · TACKLE when defending
    QSpecial — when the meter is full
    P / ESCPause · R rematch on results
    CONTROLS — MOBILE
    STICKLeft joystick moves your player
    PASSAttack: fire a pass · Defense: switch player
    SHOOTAttack: charged strike · Defense: tackle
    SPRINTHold to burn stamina for pace
    SPECIALFires when the meter is full
    Action buttons flip between PASS | SHOOT (attacking) and SWITCH | TACKLE (defending) based on who has the ball
    GAMEPLAY
    3V3You + two mates vs the Rogue Squad — each side has a goalkeeper
    WINFirst to the most goals when the clock hits zero wins
    AIMFace a teammate and PASS — the ball flies where you point
    POWERHold SHOOT longer for a harder strike; a tap is a placed finish
    COINSWins, challenges and clean sheets pay coins that unlock more stars
    GOALKEEPERS
    AUTOKeepers dive, claim and punch on their own — you never control them
    SAVEYour keeper racks up SAVES on the results board (and challenge progress)
    CLEARAfter a grab they distribute short to a free teammate — press them early
    ZONEAI strikers stay out of the six-yard box; chip or work the angle instead
    Context-sensitive controls: the same button Passes or Switches, Shoots or Tackles, depending on who has the ball.
