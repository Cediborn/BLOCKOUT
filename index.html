HOW TO PLAY
🕹️ MOVE — Arrow Keys (or left joystick on mobile)
💨 PACE / SPRINT — hold [W] — sprinting burns stamina, jogging restores it
⚡ SHOOT — [S] hold to charge — a tap is a placed finish, full charge is a strike
🔗 PASS — [I] — the ball goes to whoever you are pointing at
🛡️ DEFEND / TACKLE — [D] — get to the ball from the front or side, never from behind
🔄 SWITCH PLAYER — [X] — jump to the best teammate
🌟 SPECIAL — [Q] when the meter is full
🏁 First to the most goals when the clock hits zero wins.
📱 Mobile: joystick moves, on-screen buttons do the rest.
